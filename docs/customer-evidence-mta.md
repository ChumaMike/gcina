# MTA: first-hand customer evidence (owner: Coordinator)

**Source:** Chuma Meyiswa, who worked in MTA (Meyiswa Training Academy, Soweto) admin from Jul 2022 to 2024. He recorded this on 12 Nov 2026 from his own experience; it is **not** an interview with MTA's owner.
**Disclosure:** MTA is run by Chuma's brother (Khanya). It is a connected **design partner**, and we say so if asked.
**Privacy:** no learner names, IDs, bank details or funder amounts, anywhere.

## What he saw (his words, lightly tidied)
1. **Tools:** invoices went through SimplePay, and payments were made "using Xero, I think" (he isn't sure which system made the payments, so on stage say "payroll and accounting software" unless confirmed).
2. **Who gets paid:** learners were paid only if they were **present all the days and did their work**. The **facilitator sends the attendance register** to the owner, and the **owner approves who gets paid and how much**.
3. **What went wrong:** "**Sometimes they paid someone twice.** True story." No changed-banking-details incidents that he knows of.

## What this means for Gcina (Coordinator's decisions)
- **Duplicate payments are a real, observed problem at our design partner.** Policy rule 6 (duplicate → reject) and inv-004 are now a **headline demo moment**, not a cosmetic extra. Rule 6 should match on the same payee + same amount + same reference/period, not just the same invoice id. (L: confirm or propose.)
- **The approval model matches what MTA does today:** a human (the owner) approves a list (the register), and payment follows. Gcina's version: the owner sets the rules once; the agent pays what matches the approved register and limits; anything else waits for the owner.
- **Fraud (changed banking details)** stays our lead risk from the *market* evidence (SCA "you pay twice" cases, bank warnings), and **double payment** is the lead pain from the *customer*. Say both, honestly sourced.
- **Stretch only (after 23:00 is green):** a fixture `stipend-register.txt` with a facilitator's attendance list → the agent pays only learners marked present all days. Don't start it unless C says go.

## How to say it on stage (P)
> "I worked in admin at MTA, a training academy in Soweto. Payments were approved by hand from the facilitator's attendance register, and sometimes **the same person got paid twice**. Gcina's agent pays what the owner approved, and the ledger refuses anything twice, anything over the limit, or anything to an account the owner never approved."
