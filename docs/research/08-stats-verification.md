## Safe stage wording (survivors only)

1. **Deloitte:** "In Deloitte's latest CFO Signals survey, 54% of big-company CFOs said putting AI agents into finance is a transformation priority." (Q4 2025, 200 North American CFOs, US$1bn+.)
2. **PYMNTS:** "In a July 2025 PYMNTS survey of 60 US CFOs at billion-dollar companies, not one would give an AI agent substantial or full access to systems like payments. Only 8% would give moderate access."
3. **Wakefield/Maximor:** "A Wakefield survey of 100 US mid-market CFOs, paid for by the AI vendor Maximor, found 88% already use an agentic AI tool, but only 14% completely trust AI with accounting data on its own, and 97% say human oversight is critical."
4. **AFP:** "Three in four US organisations (74%) were hit by business email compromise attempts or losses in 2025, up from 63% in 2024 (AFP, 465 treasury professionals)."
5. **APQC:** "Benchmarks put duplicate or erroneous payments at about 1 to 2 in every 100 payments a business makes (APQC)." Do not say "rand in every R100". Leave out 1,686 and keep the 1.5% median only if you add "according to a Corpay summary of APQC 2024".
6. **SABRIC:** "South Africans lost R2.4 billion to digital banking crime in 2025, up from about R1.9 billion, across 110,074 incidents, mostly through social engineering (SABRIC)." Never call it BEC.
7. **Treasury:** "In 2024/25, national and provincial government paid 464,188 supplier invoices late, worth R43.6 billion, and R18.2 billion was still unpaid after 30 days at year end."
8. **SAnews:** "In just the third quarter of 2025/26, 89,499 invoices worth R10.8 billion were paid late."
9. **FBI IC3:** "The FBI logged US$3.05 billion in business email compromise losses from 24,768 complaints in 2025."
10. **Intengo Imoto:** "In June 2025 the Supreme Court of Appeal held that a buyer who paid R290,000 into a fraudster's account still owed the seller. The payer carries the risk. *Intengo Imoto v Zoutpansberg Motor Wholesalers* [2025] ZASCA 93."
11. **Hawarden:** "In *ENS v Hawarden* [2024] ZASCA 90, a home buyer who paid into a hacked account lost her R5.5 million claim against the sellers' attorneys."
12. **SBI:** "South Africa has only about 250,000 formal small businesses with employees (Small Business Institute, 2018)."
13. **2.6m SMMEs:** drop it, or say "about 2.6 million small businesses, most of them informal (OECD 2022)" only after opening the OECD page.
