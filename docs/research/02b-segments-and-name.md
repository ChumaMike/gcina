### 4. Spaza shops and wholesalers

**How they pay today.** Cash dominates.
- In a 150-owner study in Tlhabane, Rustenburg, 95.9% preferred cash, and cash-only supplier deliveries kept cash floats essential. Preprints.org, Oct 2026, not peer reviewed. https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202610.0349 (snippet)
- In a 51-owner Western Cape study by On the Dot (2026), 49% collect all their own stock. Same search set (snippet). Small sample.
- In the BFA Global case study of Yebo Fresh and Vuleka, stock is returned if the owner has no cash on delivery. https://bfaglobal.com/digital-spazas/insights/digital-innovations-to-build-the-resilience-of-south-african-spazas-the-case-of-yebo-fresh-and-vuleka/ (snippet)
- B2B ordering players found: Vuleka (group buying, tracks orders and payments), Yebo Fresh, Kweza, Spazapp, Last Mile for BoP, and Shoprite Cash & Carry online bulk ordering. **Kwik Spaza, Omnisient, Spaza Eats and Tradeport: not found** in this pass.

**The 2024–2025 registration drive.** It followed child deaths from contaminated food bought at spazas.
- Over 42,000 applications were received and 19,386 approved, according to CoGTA Minister Hlabisa, Dec 2024. https://www.ecr.co.za/news/news/hlabisa-42-000-applications-made-register-spazas/ (fetched)
- The deadline was extended to 28 Feb 2025. https://www.sanews.gov.za/node/72511 (snippet)
- Gauteng had 17,617 applications by 28 Feb 2025, per IOL. https://www.iol.co.za/the-star/news/gauteng-sees-surge-in-spaza-shop-registrations-as-province-grapples-with-foodborne-illness-disaster-24f43c96-e15a-49ec-87ca-bbd5f8c191f0 (snippet)
- Death tolls differ by source (22 to 41). Don't quote a death figure.

**Fit with Gcina:** this segment is weak for now. Spazas pay suppliers in cash, collect their own stock and rarely receive emailed invoices with banking details. Changed-banking-details fraud needs EFT plus emailed invoices. Spazas belong in the "later" column, perhaps through a wholesaler or B2B app partner.

## Part 2: Name check, "Gcina"

### Meaning
*Gcina* is an isiZulu/isiXhosa verb meaning "keep, preserve". Babel Free dictionary also lists "end, finish, do finally". https://babelfree.com/dictionary/zu/meaning-of-gcina. As a personal name it is common; one name site glosses it as "last one", as in Nokugcina, the full name of storyteller Gcina Mhlophe. https://www.babynology.com/name-in-news/Gcina_443.html. Watch the "end/finish" sense: it does no harm here, but be ready if a judge asks.

### Pronunciation
- "Gc" is a voiced (breathy) dental click: the "tsk" sound of disapproval, voiced, then "-ee-na". https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/Help:IPA/Zulu
- isiZulu and isiXhosa speakers say it with ease. Most English, Afrikaans and international speakers (including Cape Town judges) will say "Guh-SEE-na" or "Jee-na" without a cue.
- **One-line guide for the slide:** *Gcina (say it "!GEE-nah", the "Gc" is a soft click, like a gentle "tsk"; non-click speakers can say "GEE-nah")*. It means "keep, protect".
- **Fallback tagline:** *"Gcina: keep what's yours."* Alternative: *"Gcina: your supplier payments, kept safe."*

### Verdict: **CAUTION, close to CLASH for a public SA launch**
It is fine as a hackathon project name. But an FSCA-registered funeral-cover brand already trades as "Gcina" on gcina.co.za. It sells to the same mass-market SA audience, in the same regulated financial-services space. That is a real trade-mark and confusion risk once Gcina handles money. Before going public after the hackathon:
1. Run a CIPC trade-mark search for "GCINA" in classes 9, 36 and 42.
2. Have a compound name ready, such as "Gcina Pay" or "Gcina AP", or another word. Note that `gcinapay.co.za` had no DNS on 12 Nov 2026; that is not proof it is unregistered.
3. Don't register anything yet. That is Chuma's decision.
